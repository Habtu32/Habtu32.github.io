How I build data products

The Medical Telegram Data Warehouse, end to end: from raw public posts to an analytics API, scheduled and containerized.

Extract and load

Posts and images from public channels are collected with Telethon and loaded into PostgreSQL as raw data.

Transform and enrich

dbt turns raw tables into tested analytical models, and YOLO object detection adds what appears in each image.

Serve and schedule

FastAPI exposes the results for analysis. Dagster runs the whole pipeline, and Docker makes it reproducible.
